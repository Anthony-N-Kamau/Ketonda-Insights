    The workflow

    Three steps from a paragraph to a decision.

    No dashboards to configure, no model to train. The analysis is a structured estimate you can argue with — which is the point.

    01

    Describe the plan

    One paragraph is enough: what you are building, the capital behind it, the timeline and the market you are selling into.

    02

    Read the ranked exposures

    Every risk gets a probability, an impact and an exposure score. The ledger ranks them, so the first row is the one to fix.

    03

    Stress it, keep it, share it

    Scale every probability up or down, hold versions side by side, and export a brief for whoever signs the cheque.

    Methodology

    The arithmetic is on the page, not in a black box.

    Probabilities and impacts are model estimates, and we say so. What is not estimated is the way they combine: five fixed rules, published in full, applied the same way to every plan.

    Read the full method →
    Scoring rules v2026.1
    exposure
    probability × impact ÷ 100
    score
    Σ exposure, capped at 100
    capital at risk
    capital × weighted exposure
    bands
    <20 low · <45 moderate · <70 elevated · ≥70 critical
    stress
    every probability × (1 + s)
