Methodology

No black boxes. Just four rules.

AI estimates each risk's probability and impact from your description. Everything after that — score, severity, capital at risk — follows fixed, documented rules.

Exposure
P × I

Probability × impact, per risk. A 50% chance of a 20% loss is 10 points.

Overall score
Σ E ≤ 100

Sum of exposures, capped at 100. Under 20 Low, under 45 Moderate, under 70 Elevated, else Critical.

Severity
≥10 · ≥4

Exposure of 10 or more is High, 4 or more Medium, otherwise Low.

Capital at risk
ΣE → ΣI₍ₚ≥₅₀₎

From the probability-weighted total up to the combined impact of all risks at 50% or more.
